areeso privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026 · areeso is a Chrome extension by Himani Shah

The short version. areeso has no server of its own and collects nothing about you. Your photos, measurements and pictures stay on your computer. The only time anything leaves it is when you press See it on you: your photos and the shop's product photo are sent to OpenAI or fal, using your own key, to make the picture.

What areeso keeps, and where

Everything below is stored on your computer, inside Chrome, and nowhere else:

  • the photos of you that you add (up to five);
  • your measurements, if you enter them (all optional);
  • your try-on pictures and favourites (the Closet);
  • your settings, and the OpenAI or fal key you paste in. The key is saved on your computer as you typed it, not encrypted;
  • a short activity log of what the extension did, with no photos, keys or measurements in it. It is cleared when you close the browser.

What leaves your computer, and when

  • When you press "See it on you": your photos and the shop's product photo are sent to OpenAI (api.openai.com) or fal (fal.run), whichever key you added, along with an instruction to put you in the shop's photo. The picture comes back to your computer. Each service handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy; areeso does not see or store it.
  • Size charts: to suggest a size, areeso asks the shop's own website for its size chart, the same way your browser does when you view the page.
  • Your measurements are never sent anywhere. They are used on your computer only, to compare against the size chart.

What areeso reads on a page

To do its job, areeso reads the product name, photos and size chart of the shop page you are on, on your computer. It does not collect your browsing history, and it sends nothing about the pages you visit to areeso or anyone else, apart from what is described above.

What we do not do

  • No areeso account, server, analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.
  • We do not use your data for anything other than making your try-ons and size suggestions.

Deleting your data

Open the You tab and press Delete all my data: this removes your photos, measurements, Closet, settings and key from your computer. You can also delete single try-ons in the Closet. Removing the extension from Chrome removes everything it stored. Pictures held by OpenAI or fal are governed by their policies; check those services if you want them removed.

Children

areeso is not directed at children under 13.
